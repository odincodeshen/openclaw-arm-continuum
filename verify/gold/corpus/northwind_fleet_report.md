# Northwind sensor fleet -- quarterly report (sample)

All names, sites and figures in this report are made up for testing.

## Deployment

By the end of the quarter the fleet had 1,240 sensors in service across 31 sites. Coastal
sites account for 412 of them. The newest site, Harbour North, went live on 9 September.

## Reliability

Average uptime for the quarter was 99.2 percent. The main failure mode was connector
corrosion at coastal sites, which caused 17 of the 23 outages. Inland sites had no
corrosion faults.

## Maintenance

The battery packs are replaced every 18 months. Firmware 4.7.2 was rolled out to every
sensor in August; it lowers the reporting interval to 90 seconds.

## People and budget

The engineering lead for the fleet is Priya Okafor. The maintenance budget for next year is
86,500 pounds. The next external audit is scheduled for 14 March 2027.
